10 Year Anniversary

On October 30, DESERT X returns to the Coachella Valley for its tenth-anniversary exhibition, curated by Neville Wakefield and Kaitlin Garcia-Maestas. For this edition, the exhibition opens in the fall and continues through winter and spring. New site-responsive works will evolve with the desert, inviting visitors to return throughout the season and see how the works change with the landscape.

Opening Weekend
Oct 28–31, 2027

Public exhibition
Oct 30, 2027–
May 7, 2028

The exhibition is free and open to all. Participating artists and public programs will be announced in the coming months.
